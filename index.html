04 / Study design

Data provenance and limitations

CDC PLACES

County-level modeled adult hearing-disability prevalence. These estimates are not counts of diagnosed individuals.

CDC PLACES website

NOAA Storm Events

County-based tornado event records, 2023–2025. A tornado may be recorded in more than one county.

NOAA Storm Events

FEMA National Risk Index

Hazard-specific modeled expected annual loss in dollars. Not a direct measure of emergency communication accessibility.

FEMA National Risk Index

Public-source review

A purposive seven-county desk review; findings may not generalize to all 46 counties. Missing documentation is not proof that an accommodation is absent.

ADA effective communication guidance

Disaster Access Lab · Educational research prototype · No alerts, warnings, or individualized risk estimates.
